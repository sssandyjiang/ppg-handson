How to measure
  1. Choose your pair number and the condition (start with Rest).
  2. Hold the phone and cover the whole rear lens with the pad of your fingertip. Press gently.
  3. Your partner shines their phone's flashlight on your fingernail.
  4. When the dot turns green, your partner presses "Record 30 s". Hold still, and count your own pulse (neck or wrist) during the same 30 s.
  5. Type your count, press "Save to my list", and start the next condition.
  6. No phone? Open this page on a laptop and cover the webcam the same way.
How it works

Each frame, the page averages the red brightness of the image. More blood in your fingertip absorbs more light, so the image gets slightly darker with every beat. The page removes slow drift, finds the peaks, and turns the gaps between peaks into beats per minute. A beat is counted when the wave reaches the threshold and comes at least the minimum gap after the previous beat — the two sliders. If your finger left the lens or the beats came at a very uneven pace, the page says no pulse instead of guessing. Full code: view this page's source — the first script is the whole processing chain.
